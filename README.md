# Endless Tales 2 for Windows

**Endless Tales 2** is a fantasy RPG full of quests, towns, battles and stories, and now it's on
Windows for the first time.

Live your own story: start as a humble peasant in Lisport, talk to the people you meet, take on
quests, fight monsters, trade in shops, craft and cook, buy a house, ride a mount, and explore a
whole world, one region at a time.

You can play it with a **screen reader and the keyboard alone**, or with a mouse.

## Download

Get it from the **[Releases page](https://github.com/swarup-developer/endless-tales-windows-port/releases/latest)**.
There are two versions; pick the one you like:

- **Installer** (`EndlessTales2-Setup-….exe`): run it and follow the steps. You get a Start menu
  entry, a desktop shortcut if you want one, and an uninstaller. No administrator rights needed.
- **Portable** (`EndlessTales2-…-portable.zip`): unzip the whole folder anywhere (even a USB stick)
  and open `EndlessTales2.exe`. Your saves stay inside that folder.

Both have everything inside; you don't need to install anything else.
You need Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) and about 750 MB of free space.

**Windows warning the first time?** Windows may say it "protected your PC" because the game is new.
Choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

**What's new:** see the **[Changelog](CHANGELOG.md)**.

## Made for blind and visually impaired players

- **Talks to you through NVDA**, and to your braille display too. No screen reader running? The
  Windows voice reads everything instead.
- **No "button, button, button".** Menus say only what matters, like "Actions, 2 of 5".
- **Conversations say who is speaking first**, like "Herald: Hello, Tester".
- **Nothing is missed:** places, events, rewards and pop-ups are read to you, and **R** repeats the
  last thing any time.
- **S tells you your status** (health, mana, gold, level, day, time and place) whenever you want.
- **Name entry is spoken letter by letter.**
- **Options take effect right away**, so you hear the change while you adjust a volume or the speed.
- **Sighted and low-vision players** get a bright gold highlight on the current choice, and full
  mouse support.

## Keys

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| Up / Down arrows (or Tab) | Move through the choices |
| Enter or Space | Choose |
| Escape or Backspace | Go back, or open the game menu |
| Left / Right arrows | Change a setting |
| Home / End | First / last choice |
| Page Up / Page Down | Jump between sections |
| Shift + a letter | Next choice starting with that letter |
| R | Repeat |
| S | Your status |
| C | Character sheet: stats, quests, inventory |
| I | Inventory |
| Ctrl | Stop speech |
| F1 | Keyboard help |
| F11 | Full screen |

## Languages

English, Español and Português. Change it in **Options → Language**.

## Your saves

The game saves by itself, and you can also choose **Save game** in the game menu (Escape).

- **Installer version:** saves are in `%APPDATA%\EndlessTales2`. They stay safe even if you uninstall the game.
- **Portable version:** saves are in the `saves` folder inside the game folder.

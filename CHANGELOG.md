# Changelog

Everything new in Endless Tales 2 for Windows.

## Version 2.0.3 (10 November 2026): the first Windows release

Endless Tales 2 has never had a Windows version before. This is the first one, with the whole game
on your PC.

### The whole game, on Windows

- **The full adventure:** the same story, quests, people, places and items as the original game.
- **Explore the world:** travel between regions, towns and places, with art for every location
  that changes between day and night.
- **Talk to everyone:** every conversation and reply option from the original game.
- **Events and choices:** story events show up as scenes, and your decisions matter.
- **Actions:** gather, work, rest and more, with rewards, gold and experience.
- **Turn-based battles:** attack, use skills and items, pick your target, or escape. Critical hits
  give you a quick "press Space now!" moment, which you can switch to automatic in Options.
- **Shops and stables:** buy and sell items, and buy mounts.
- **Inventory and equipment:** use, equip, take off and discard items, with filters for
  consumables and other items.
- **Skills and attributes:** learn skills, use them outside battle, and spend skill points on your
  attributes.
- **Crafting, cooking and alchemy:** combine up to three ingredients. The game tells you what they
  make and your chance of success.
- **Houses:** rest in your own house, and keep items in its chest.
- **Mounts:** ride a mount, and carry more in its saddlebag.
- **Map and bestiary:** see each region's places and roads, and the creatures that live there.
- **Quests, level ups and defeat:** all the pop-ups, rewards and messages from the original.
- **Several characters:** continue your last character, choose another one, or delete one.
- **Autosave**, plus Save game in the menu.

### Made for blind and visually impaired players

- The game speaks **directly through NVDA**, and to a braille display too.
- No screen reader running? The **Windows voice** reads everything instead.
- You can **play the whole game with the keyboard**, from the first screen to the last.
- Menus **never say "button"**. You hear only the choice and its place, like "Actions, 2 of 5",
  and you can switch the "2 of 5" part off.
- In conversations you hear **who is speaking first**, like "Herald: Hello, Tester".
- **Places, events, rewards and pop-ups are read to you** automatically.
- **R repeats** the last text, **S says your status**, **C opens your character sheet**,
  **I opens your inventory**, **Ctrl stops speech**, and **F1 lists every key**.
- When a pop-up closes, or you come back to the game window, it **tells you where you are**.
- **Name entry is spoken letter by letter**, and Backspace says which letter it removed.
- **Turning speech off is never silent:** the game says how to turn it back on.
- Sighted and low-vision players get a **bright gold highlight** and **full mouse support**.

### Options

- **Speech output:** Automatic (NVDA, otherwise the Windows voice), Screen reader only, Windows
  voice, or Off.
- **Windows voice speed**, from slower to faster.
- **Say position in lists** ("2 of 5") on or off.
- **Sound when moving through lists** on or off.
- **Critical hits:** press Space quickly, or automatic.
- **Music volume** and **sound effects volume**, read as percentages.
- **Text speed on screen**, from slow to instant.
- **Full screen** (also F11).
- **Language:** English, Español or Português.
- Every option takes effect **right away** and is remembered for next time.

### Three languages

- English, Spanish and Portuguese, for the story **and** every menu, button, help line and message
  of the Windows edition.
- Switching language in Options changes everything immediately. You don't need to restart.
- Fixed some mistakes in the original game's Spanish text, for example "Idade" is now "Edad", and
  the hostage event says "¡Éxito!" / "¡Fracaso!".

### Two ways to install

- **Installer:** Start menu entry, optional desktop shortcut and an uninstaller. No administrator
  rights needed. The setup itself is in English, Spanish or Portuguese.
- **Portable:** unzip and play from any folder or USB stick. Your saves stay in that folder.
- Both include everything; you don't need to install anything else.
- Uninstalling never deletes your saves.

### Fixes before release

- The options menu now reads every setting correctly, and Done takes you back to where you came from.
- Changing the language no longer leaves a duplicate main menu behind the options.
- F11 full screen no longer switches itself back on and off.
- Closing the character sheet, help or menu now tells you where you are, instead of going silent.
- Crafting shows the right button (Cook, Mix or Craft) in every language.
